# Rem: "Remind Me of Everything" Feature Catalog

Every useful feature from the best reminder, task, alarm, habit, medicine, bill and family apps, collected into one app. Each feature notes **where the idea comes from**, so you know it's proven.

Apps studied: TickTick, Todoist, Google Tasks/Keep, Microsoft To Do, Any.do, Samsung Reminder, BZ Reminder, Galarm, Alarmy, Due (iOS), Remember The Milk, Memorigi, Structured, Medisafe, MyTherapy, Bills Reminder / Timely Bills, Life360, Cozi, OurHome, Habitica, Loop Habit Tracker, Forest, Countdown apps and Birthday apps.

> ★ = Rem's unique twist (no single app has it).

## Rem's pitch in one line
> **"If it has a date, a place or a habit, Rem remembers it, and it won't let you forget."**

## The 5 features that make Rem stand out
1. **Remind anything**: one app for tasks, medicine, bills, birthdays, documents, vehicle, home, pets and people.
2. **Can't-miss reminders**: escalation, nag mode, proof-to-dismiss, and a battery-saver guide.
3. **Smart triggers**: location, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, app-open, weather, leave-by time.
4. **Family care**: remind others, and alert a caregiver if someone misses medicine.
5. **Private and free**: offline, no account, no AI APIs, with the core features free.
